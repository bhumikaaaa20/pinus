Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

We stripped away the noise. PinUs does one thing perfectly: keeping you connected with the people who matter.

Real-time GPS

See your partner's live location on the map, updated every 10-30 seconds. Simulated in this demo.

No Ghost Mode

Everyone's location is always visible. Subscribing is a conscious act of trust — no hiding, no excuses.

Private Circle

Only paired users can see each other. 2 for couples, up to 6 for families. No strangers, ever.

SOS Alert

One-tap emergency alert sends your live location and battery status to all paired members instantly.

Name Labels

See who's where at a glance. Each person's name appears right on the map above their live pin.

Instant Unlink

Leave any time. One tap stops sharing your location permanently. No guilt, no questions, full control.

How it works

Three steps to stay connected

Getting started with PinUs takes less than two minutes.

1

Sign up with your phone

Enter your phone number and verify with OTP. Your phone number is your identity — simple and secure.

2

Share your invite code

Get a unique 6-digit code. Send it to your partner or family members. They enter it, and you're paired.

3

See each other, always

Both of you appear on a shared private map with your names. Real-time, accurate, and completely private.
